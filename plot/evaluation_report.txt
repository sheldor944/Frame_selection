METRIC INTERPRETATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. SCBI (Score-Coverage Balanced Index):
   Combined metric balancing score quality and temporal coverage.
   Higher is better. Range: [0, 1]

2. Score Quality (S):
   Ratio of selected frame scores to optimal top-M scores.
   Measures how well high-quality frames are captured.
   Higher is better. Range: [0, 1]

3. Coverage (C):
   Measures temporal distribution uniformity.
   Higher means more evenly distributed frames across video.
   Higher is better. Range: [0, 1]

4. Diversity (D):
   Variance in selected frame scores (normalized).
   Higher means more diverse quality selection.
   Context-dependent: not always better. Range: [0, 1]

5. Efficiency (E):
   Compression ratio (frames selected vs available).
   Higher means fewer frames selected (more compression).
   Higher is better for efficiency. Range: [0, 1]

6. Balance (B):
   Harmonic mean of Score Quality and Coverage.
   Penalizes algorithms that excel in only one dimension.
   Higher is better. Range: [0, 1]
